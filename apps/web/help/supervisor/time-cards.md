Hours come from the daily report: each person on the day has a time card. They wait for approval grouped by day and job.

## Approving them

On the home queue, **Time cards to approve** lists the groups. Open one to see each person's hours, or tap **Approve all** for the group when they match the day.

## Where hours go

Approved time cards are the payroll record. A person's own hours also show on their home; the driller's card fills from their own checklist and log times, so most cards arrive already right.
