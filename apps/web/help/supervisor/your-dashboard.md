A supervisor's home is the blaster's home with the company in reach: your own days first, everyone's days one tap away, and the approvals waiting for you under Admin.

## Where things are

- **Work days › Everyone** — every crew's days, with search. The switch is remembered on the device.
- **Admin › Approvals** — filed days waiting for a decision, and the time cards that came with them.
- **Records** — the company's record book: every filed day, log, checklist and incident, with its PDF.
