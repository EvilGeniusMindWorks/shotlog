A filed day is a signed PDF and the live record behind it. Review either.

## From Approvals

**Admin › Approvals** lists filed days oldest first, each with the facts that matter — job, shots, pounds, crew — and **Review**, which opens the day. The PDF is one tap from there.

![Approvals](approvals.png)

## Two copies of a day

When two devices worked the same day without signal, the day shows **Two copies of this day** with **Merge into this one**. Nothing is lost; you pick the copy to keep and the other is folded in.

## Locked fields

A filed day is locked. If it must change, send it back to the blaster with a note rather than editing it yourself: the fix becomes version 2 and the history stays honest.
