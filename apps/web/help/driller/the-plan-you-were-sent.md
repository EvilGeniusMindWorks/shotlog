The blaster lays out the pattern — rows, columns, depths — and sends it to the drillers. On your side it arrives as a drill log with the pattern already on it.

## Where it shows

- On your home, the **Today's drill log** tile names it.
- The **Drilling** tab lists every plan sent to you: what is assigned, what is ready to drill, what is done. Open one to log holes.

![The Drilling tab](drilling.png)

## More than one driller

Every driller on the job sees the same plan. You split the pattern between you the way you always have — on the ground. Each of you logs the holes you drilled; the blaster sees all of them on one grid with your initials.

## No plan here

The blaster has not sent one yet. Ask them, or start a drill-only day from **+** if the job has no blaster today.
