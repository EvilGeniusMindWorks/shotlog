The drill log is the blaster's pattern, hole by hole, in the same shape they laid it out. You record what actually went into the ground.

## Logging holes

1. **Tap the holes you drilled** on the grid. Tap a row handle (R1, R2…) to take a whole row; **Select all open** takes everything not yet logged.
2. Tap **Log N as planned**. Two taps for a clean row.
3. Something different in the ground — water, a void, a change of depth? Tap **Log with changes** and enter what you found. **Wet** and **hazard** are one tap each; the blaster sees them before loading.
4. A hole you did not drill: **Mark skipped**. Skipping is normal, not an apology; a blank hole is the thing to avoid.

![The drill log](drill-log.png)

## Holes the plan never had

Drilled an extra hole? Type its number and log it. It sits below the pattern as **off-plan** so the blaster knows.

## Your rig

Pick the rig at the top of the log. It ties the day's hours to the machine, and the shop's clock reads it.

## The logged list

Under the grid, your logged holes newest first — the latest eight, with **Show all** for the rest. Tap one to change it.
