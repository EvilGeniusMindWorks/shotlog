The rig checklist is the paper walk-around, once a day per machine — and the rig's own odometer, too: it carries the hours the machine starts the day with and the hours it stops with. It needs no job and no plan. Defects you note become shop tickets on their own.

## Which rig

The rig is the first question. The quick picks are the ones that matter today — the rig on your drill log, the last one you filed, your usual — and **All rigs** opens the whole fleet with search. Two rigs today means two checklists: file one, then tap **Start a checklist for another rig** to pick the next — nothing about the first is shared with it.

## One rig, two jobs in a day

A checklist belongs to a rig **at a job, on a day** — not just to the day. Move a rig from one job to a second job the same day and it gets its own second checklist there, prefilled with the starting hours from the rig's last reading and the morning's walk-around answers carried over, marked "carried from this morning's checklist at <job>." **Stop for the day** always records its reading on the checklist for the job you're at — not the first one you filed.

## Hour meter first

The field starts with the machine's last reading, and says so underneath. Read the gauge: if it matches, leave it; if not, type what you see. The starting hours drive the 50-hour service clock and the shop's ledger. A number going backwards is ignored, so a wrong one cannot roll a machine back. The drill log no longer asks for a meter reading — this checklist is the only place hours are entered; the log just names the rig.

## Stop for the day

When you're done with a rig, open its checklist and tap **Stop for the day** to enter the meter reading now — that closes out its hours for the shop's ledger and the daily report's equipment hours. A rig with no stop reading shows as running until someone enters one, today or the next morning.

## The walk-around

Every item starts ticked. Tap only what is **N/A** or **not done**. The 50-hour / weekly section is there when it is due; the app works out when from the hours.

## Repairs and out of service

- **Repairs needed** — write what you found. The shop sees it as a ticket the moment you file.
- **Out of service** — tick it, enter the meter reading now, and the rig is pulled from the fleet until the shop clears it; a repair ticket opens for the shop. Use it when the machine should not run.

## Sign and file

Sign, then **File checklist**. The office copy is filed for you; the ticket, if any, is in the shop's queue.

If the office copy fails (the screen says so and offers **Try again**), the checklist itself is still saved. Open the rig's checklist page later and tap **File the office copy** to send it.
