The rig checklist is the paper walk-around, once a day per machine. It needs no job and no plan. Defects you note become shop tickets on their own.

## Which rig

The rig is the first question. The quick picks are the ones that matter today — the rig on your drill log, the last one you filed, your usual — and **All rigs** opens the whole fleet with search. Two rigs today? File one, then pick the other.

## Hour meter first

Read the meter and type it. The starting hours drive the 50-hour service clock and the shop's ledger. A typo going backwards is ignored, so a wrong number cannot roll a machine back.

![The rig checklist](checklist.png)

## The walk-around

Every item starts ticked. Tap only what is **N/A** or **not done**. The 50-hour / weekly section is there when it is due; the app works out when from the hours.

## Repairs and out of service

- **Repairs needed** — write what you found. The shop sees it as a ticket the moment you file.
- **Out of service** — tick it and the rig is pulled from the fleet until the shop clears it. Use it when the machine should not run.

## Sign and file

Sign, then **File checklist**. The office copy is filed for you; the ticket, if any, is in the shop's queue.
