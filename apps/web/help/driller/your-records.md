**My records** is everything you filed — drill logs, rig checklists, drill-only days — newest first, with the PDF.

## What is there

Each record with its status: filed, accepted, or sent back. Tap one to read it or print it. The search box finds a record by job, date or type.

## Sign once

Your signature is drawn once in **My profile**; after that every sign-off is one tap.
