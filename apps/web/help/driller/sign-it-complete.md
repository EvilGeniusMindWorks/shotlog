When the pattern is done, sign the log. That hands it to the blaster, who accepts it before loading.

## Signing

Scroll to the signature at the bottom, sign, and tap **Mark complete** right there (it is at the top too, if you prefer). The app asks for the rig's **end-of-day hour meter** once, prefilled from the morning — it closes the day's hours for the shop.

## What the blaster sees

The day's Drilling phase turns **ready to review**. The blaster reviews the grid — your initials on every hole, wet and hazard holes coloured, skips and off-plan holes marked — and accepts it. Accepting locks the holes.

## A log you did not sign

It waits on your home under **Yesterday needs you** until you do. If the blaster sends a log back with a note, it comes back the same way.
