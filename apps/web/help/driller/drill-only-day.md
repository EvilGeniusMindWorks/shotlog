Some days there is no blaster: you drill ahead, or a job is drilling only. You can run the whole day yourself.

## Starting it

Tap **+** on your home. Pick the job (recent jobs are one tap), the date, and **Drill only** as the type of work. If you're first to open this job on this date, the day starts with **the card** — type of work, temperature, weather, ground and the rest — then your trio: checklist, drill log, hours. A blaster who opens the same day later sees your card as a fact sheet and taps to confirm it, rather than filling in their own.

## Filing it

At the end of the day, sign the drill log complete and file the day. It goes to the office like any other day, without a blaster's involvement. If a blaster opens the same day at that job, your drilling joins it — nothing is typed twice. Need a daily report for hours or equipment? Tap **Start daily report** on that tab — it doesn't exist until you do.
