Some days there is no blaster: you drill ahead, or a job is drilling only. You can run the whole day yourself.

## Starting it

Tap **+** on your home. Pick the job (recent jobs are one tap), the date, and **Drill only** as the type of work. The day opens with your trio: checklist, drill log, hours.

## Filing it

At the end of the day, sign the drill log complete and file the day. It goes to the office like any other day, without a blaster's involvement. If a blaster opens the same day at that job, your drilling joins it — nothing is typed twice.
