You get an email from ShotLog with a button. That button is your account — it works once and only for you.

## Setting up

1. Tap the button in the email. You see **Welcome, <your name>** and a place for a password.
2. Choose a password and type it twice. Tap **Create my account**. You are signed in.
3. Pick a **6-digit PIN**. This is what opens ShotLog on your device from now on, with or without signal. See [Your PIN](/help/start-here/your-pin).
4. A short welcome shows once, then a walkthrough of your screens. You can skip it and run it again any time from the ? menu.

![The sign-in screen](sign-in.png)

## Signing in again

On this device you will not see the sign-in screen again — the PIN opens the app. On a new device, sign in with your email and password once; after that the PIN takes over there too.

> **Link expired or already used?** Ask whoever invited you to send a new one. If you set a password and forgot it, tap **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen.
