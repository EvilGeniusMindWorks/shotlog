Everything you do in ShotLog is saved on your device first. Signal is only needed to share it — with the office, with the drillers, with your other devices.

## The chip in the header

| Chip | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Synced** | Everything on this device has been sent, and everything the company has is here. |
| **Syncing** | Sending or receiving. Keep working; it finishes on its own. |
| **Offline** | No connection. Keep working; it sends when signal returns. |
| **Downloading company data — 42%** | A new device is fetching the company's records for the first time. Wait for it before relying on lists. |

![The sync chip](sync-chip.png)

## What happens when you are out of signal

- New days, logs, readings and signatures are all kept on the device.
- Filing a day works offline: the PDFs are made on the device and upload later. The day shows **Filed · waiting to upload** until then.
- When signal returns, the chip goes **Syncing** then **Synced**. You do not have to do anything.

## When two people edit the same day

It happens: a blaster and a supervisor on the same day, both without signal. When both copies arrive, the day shows a **merge strip** to the author and to supervision so nothing is silently lost.

## If it never says Synced

Tap the chip. The panel shows what is queued and when it last synced. If it is stuck for more than a few minutes with signal, see [It says "Syncing" and never "Synced"](/help/troubleshooting/stuck-syncing).
