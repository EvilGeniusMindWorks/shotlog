Your profile is what sign-off needs: your name, your blasting licenses, and your signature. ShotLog fills these in for you on every log, so set them once.

## Licenses

Add one license per state: state, license number, expiry date. When you start a job, ShotLog picks the license for that job's state automatically. A license close to expiry shows a countdown so you and the office see it coming.

## Signature

Draw your signature once on the screen, with a finger or a stylus. It is used at sign-off on the blasting log and the drill log. You can redraw it any time.

![My profile](profile.png)

## Why the app nags

Until your license and signature are on file, the app reminds you on the Dashboard, and it will not let a shot be signed off. That is deliberate: a log without a real license and signature is not a record.
