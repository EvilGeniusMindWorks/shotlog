Your PIN is six digits you choose once. It unlocks ShotLog on your device the way a phone's lock screen does, and it works without signal.

## Why a PIN and not the password

In the field there is often no signal to check a password against. The PIN is checked on the device itself, so the app opens anywhere. The PIN belongs to **your account**, not to the device: the same six digits work on a tablet and a phone.

![The PIN screen](pin.png)

## Changing it

Open **Settings › You › My profile** and tap **Change PIN**. You will set a new one the next time the app locks.

## Forgot it

Tap **Forgot PIN** on the PIN screen. You sign in with your email and password (this needs signal), then set a new PIN. Nothing on the device is lost.

## Other people's devices

Signing out forgets your PIN on that device. If someone else signs in on a tablet you used, they set their own PIN; yours stays yours.
