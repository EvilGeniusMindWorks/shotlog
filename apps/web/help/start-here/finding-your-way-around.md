ShotLog has a few home screens and a lot of papers under them. Two things get you anywhere: the tab bar at the bottom (the rail on a wide screen) and the arrow at the top left of every other screen.

## The roots

The tab bar's screens — Dashboard, Work days, Jobs, Records, and for drillers Drilling — are the roots. Tap one and you are at its top. Roots have no arrow.

## The arrow

Every other screen has one parent, and the arrow in its header says where it goes: **‹ Work days**, **‹ Route 3 Widening**, **‹ Blasting log**. Tap it and you go up one level — the day's papers go back to the day, the day goes back to Work days.

Came in sideways? If you opened a day from Records, from a job's page or from a card on your home, the arrow takes you back there instead, and says so: **‹ Records**. It remembers where you came from, not just where the screen belongs.

On a wide screen the header also shows the whole path — Work days ▸ Route 3 Widening · Thu, Sep 10 ▸ Blasting log ▸ Shot 1 — and every step is tappable.

## Finishing lands you forward

When you finish a paper, you land where it now lives, never back on the form:

- **File this day** lands on the day, filed.
- **Mark complete** on a drill log lands on the day, where the log reads *Signed complete*.
- **Mark the daily report done** lands on the day, where the tile reads *Done*.
- **Done** after filing a rig checklist lands on the day you started it from, or on the rig.

## Sheets

A sheet that slides up — time cards, a rig, a person, Change the date — says **Close**. Closing it leaves the screen behind it exactly as it was.

## On a phone

The Android back gesture does the same as the arrow, step for step, and closes an open sheet first. An installed app on an iPhone has no back gesture, which is why the arrow is on every screen that is not a root.
