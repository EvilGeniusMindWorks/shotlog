Most people never sign out — the PIN keeps the app yours. Sign out when a device changes hands.

## Two ways out

Open **Settings › You › My profile**.

- **Sign out** — for your own device. Your PIN is forgotten on it, but the company's data stays, so signing back in is quick.
- **Sign out & clear this device** — for a shared tablet or one you are handing to someone else. Everything ShotLog stored on the device is removed.

## Somebody else's tablet

If you sign in on a device someone else used, you set your own PIN and see your own work. Their PIN no longer works there. Nothing of theirs is shown to you unless you go looking in the company's records — and the same is true the other way around.
