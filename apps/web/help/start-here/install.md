ShotLog runs in the browser, but it is meant to be installed: an icon on the home screen, full screen, no address bar, and it opens like any other app. Installing also keeps your company's data on the device for offline work.

## Android tablet or phone (Chrome)

1. Open ShotLog in Chrome and sign in.
2. Chrome offers **Add ShotLog to Home screen** — tap it. If it does not, open Chrome's menu (⋮) and choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
3. Open ShotLog from the new icon from now on.

## iPhone or iPad (Safari)

1. Open ShotLog in Safari and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open ShotLog from the new icon from now on. Safari itself keeps a separate copy — use the icon.

![The Install card in Settings](install-card.png)

## Did it work?

Installed, ShotLog fills the whole screen with no browser bar. **Settings › Install** tells you whether this device is running the installed app.

> **Two icons?** You installed twice. Keep one and delete the other from the home screen; nothing is lost either way.
