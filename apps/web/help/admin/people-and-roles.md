A role is a bundle of capabilities: what a person may see and do. The built-in roles cover the company; custom roles cover the exceptions.

## The built-in roles

| Role | Home | Typical capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| Blaster | Field dashboard | Work days and field reports, blasting logs and shots, submit days, set up customers, sites and jobs |
| Driller | Driller home | Work days, equipment hours and checklists, complete drill logs, file time cards |
| Supervisor | Field dashboard | Everything a blaster can, plus approve and unlock days, accept drill patterns, manage the fleet |
| Mechanic | Mechanic home | Equipment hours and checklists, correct hour meters, resolve repair tickets, manage the fleet |
| Office | Office home | Approve days, file office copies, process incident claims, manage people, jobs and customers |
| Admin | Field dashboard + Admin | All of it, plus roles, company setup and the catalog |

## Custom roles

**Admin › Roles** shows each role's capabilities as switches. Make a new role from an existing one and change what it may do — "Trainee: work days but no submitting", say. People on a custom role see the home you choose for it.
