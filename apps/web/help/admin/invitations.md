Every login starts with an invitation from People.

## Sending one

On the person's row, **Invite** → their email → send. The email carries a one-time link that expires in a few days. No email address? Share the link yourself. The email's wording follows the company: a testing company sends "an invitation to try ShotLog"; the production company sends the set-up-your-account wording.

## What they see

The link asks for a password, signs them in, asks for a PIN, shows a one-time welcome and the walkthrough for their role. The guide's [Start here](/help/start-here/your-invitation) section is linked from the email.

## Housekeeping

- A used or expired link cannot be reused — send a new invitation.
- **Reset password** sends a temporary one; **Deactivate** stops the login and keeps the records.
