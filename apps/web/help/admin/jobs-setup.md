Jobs are set up from **Jobs**: customer first, then its site, then the job. Blasters can do this themselves for a small job; the office keeps the details true afterwards.

## What a job carries

Its customer and site (and everything on them), a job number assigned automatically per year, PO, status (quoted · active · on hold · complete), start and target dates, engineer of record, operation, and defaults for the day — type of work, rock, terrain, hazards, precautions.

## Keeping the list short

Jobs windowed to 15 with Show all; the search box finds any customer, site or job. A finished job is set to **complete** or archived from its page; it leaves the pickers and stays in the records.
