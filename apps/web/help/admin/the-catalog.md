The catalog is what the blaster picks from when entering explosives: manufacturers, their products, and the weight of each unit.

## Manufacturers and products

One tab per manufacturer. Each product has a name, a **unit** (case, booster, cap, foot…) and a **weight in pounds per unit** — the multiplier that turns a count into pounds on the blasting log. Fifteen products show per tab; **Show all** for the rest.

![The catalog](catalog.png)

## Adding and retiring

**New manufacturer** and **Add product** are on the page. A product no longer used is **retired**: it leaves the blaster's list but stays on every old log that named it.
