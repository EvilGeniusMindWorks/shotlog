**Admin › Company** holds what goes on every form: company name, dealer number, address, phone. Change it here and every new PDF carries it.

## Also here

The company's list of **reasons** and **document types** used in the field (for example a permit or a pre-blast survey as an attachment type), and the costing table the admin home shows.

## Setup fields

**Setup fields** opens from a card on this page. It is the whole list of facts a customer, a site or a job can carry, in six groups: who, money, compliance, the ground, emergency, the job. For each row: where it lives, whether setting up a job asks for it **at setup**, **later** or **rarely**, and whether a missing one **holds a blasting day**. A row moved to At setup appears on the New job sheet and as a tile on every job; a row that holds a blasting day shows red on the Start-work sheet for a blasting type until the site (or the customer, for insurance) carries it. Rows that are always asked, automatic, or come from the town say so instead of offering a switch. **Back to the defaults** undoes every change.
