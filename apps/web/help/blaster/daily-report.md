The daily report is the operations side of the day: who worked, how long, with what. It shares everything with the blasting log, so nothing is typed twice.

## Crew and hours

Add each person who worked. Their hours become **time cards** the supervisor and office see. A person you add here is on the blasting log's sign-off list.

## Equipment

The rigs, trucks and instruments used. A rig picked here is the one whose **checklist** and hours the day expects.

## Weather, work performed, notes

Short and plain. The office reads these when a question comes up weeks later.

![The daily report](daily-report.png)

> **Copying from the previous day** brings the crew and equipment over, so on a multi-day job the report is mostly done before you start.
