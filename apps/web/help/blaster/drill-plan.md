The drill plan is the pattern: rows and columns of holes with their depths. You build it once; the drillers log against it; the timing is built on it afterwards.

## Building the pattern

1. From the day, tap **Continue** (*Build the drill plan*) or the Drill plan phase.
2. Set **rows × columns** and the **default depth**. The grid draws itself.
3. Type a depth (and kick), then tap holes to paint them. To leave a position out, pick **⌀ No hole** first, then tap it — a gap keeps the pattern's shape. Row handles (R1, R2…) paint a whole row.
4. When the footer reads **Plan ready**, tap **Send to drillers**.

![The drill plan](drill-plan.png)

## Sending

Pick the driller or drillers. Each gets a drill log on their device with your pattern on it. The day's Drilling phase now reads *in progress* with their names and progress, and **Continue** waits for them.

> **Changing the plan after sending?** You can. The driller sees the new pattern; holes already logged stay logged.
