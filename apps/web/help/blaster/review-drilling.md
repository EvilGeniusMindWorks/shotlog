When a driller signs a log complete, the day's Drilling phase reads **ready to review**. Your review is the bridge from what was planned to what will be loaded.

## What you see

The pattern, in its own shape, one grid per shot, every hole the same size whatever the shot's width — a wide shot scrolls sideways instead of shrinking. Every hole shows who drilled it (initials), and holes the driller flagged — wet, hazard, skipped — are coloured. Holes the driller added that the plan never had sit below as **off-plan**, and the header counts them beside hazards and skipped — "24 holes · 1 driller · 1 hazards · 1 skipped · 1 off-plan".

**Grid / List** switches to every hole as a row: number, planned and drilled depth, angle, what the driller found, their comment. Tap a row for the same detail card the grid opens.

Under the pattern, each log has its line: the driller, the rig, the header they drilled to — diameter, burden, spacing, face height — and their sign-off note, so the whole of what they filled out is on this screen.

## Sending a log back

Something wrong — a row logged at one depth across, a hole missing? Tap **Send back…**, pick the log if there is more than one, write what needs fixing, and send. The driller's log reopens with your note at the top, and a line on their home says you sent it back. Nothing they logged is lost.

## Accepting

Tap **Accept**. Each driller's log is accepted on its own: accepting a second shot's log never touches a log already accepted, and the screen names when and by whom each one was accepted. The phase reads *accepted* and **Continue** offers *Confirm design & build timing*: the wiring diagram opens on the holes as drilled, so you plan the timing of real holes, not planned ones. Undrilled holes cannot be wired.

> **Drilling changed after you wired?** A driller logged more holes after you built the timing. The day flags it; one tap rebuilds the timing on the new pattern.
