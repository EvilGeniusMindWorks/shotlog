When a driller signs a log complete, the day's Drilling phase reads **ready to review**. Your review is the bridge from what was planned to what will be loaded.

## What you see

The pattern, in its own shape, one grid per shot. Every hole shows who drilled it (initials), and holes the driller flagged — wet, hazard, skipped — are coloured. Holes the driller added that the plan never had sit below as **off-plan**.

## Accepting

Tap **Accept**. The phase reads *accepted* and **Continue** offers *Confirm design & build timing*: the wiring diagram opens on the holes as drilled, so you plan the timing of real holes, not planned ones. Undrilled holes cannot be wired.

> **Drilling changed after you wired?** A driller logged more holes after you built the timing. The day flags it; one tap rebuilds the timing on the new pattern.
