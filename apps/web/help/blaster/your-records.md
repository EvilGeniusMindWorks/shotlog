**My records** is everything you filed, with its PDF, newest first.

## What is there

Each filed day, drill log and checklist, with its status: filed, approved, or sent back. Tap one to see it; the PDF opens in the app.

![My records](records.png)

## Searching

The search box finds a record by job, customer, date or type. Filters narrow by status.

## Versions

A day filed twice shows both versions. The latest is the record; the earlier one stays for the history.
