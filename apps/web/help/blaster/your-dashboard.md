The Dashboard is your day. It shows what you are working on, what is waiting for you, and nothing else unless you go looking.

## What is on it

- **Today** — the day you are working, with **Continue** for the next thing to do. No day today? The **+** button starts one.
- **Sent back** — a day the office returned, with their note. Fix it and file again.
- **My recent days** — the days you authored, newest first.
- **Reminders** — a license expiring, a missing signature, a rig checklist not filed.

![The Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## Yours first

Unasked, ShotLog shows you only what you authored. Everyone's days are one tap away: **Work days › Everyone**. The switch is remembered on the device.

## The + button

Starts work at a job: name the day if you like, pick the job, the date and the type of work, and copy from the last day there. See [Start work at a job](/help/blaster/start-work).
