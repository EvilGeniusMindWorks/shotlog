The office can return a filed day with a note. It is not a mark against you — usually a missing signature, a crew member not on the report, or a number that does not match the paper.

## What you see

The day appears on your Dashboard under **Sent back**, with the office's note, and the day itself shows a banner at the top — who sent it back, when, and their words. The day is unlocked again.

## One paper sent back

The office can also send back one paper and keep the rest: a time card, a drill log, a rig checklist. The day stays filed. Your Dashboard shows "Sent back · Time card · Lisa Vital" with the note, the day's File row reads "1 paper sent back", and the paper itself carries the note where it is fixed — the time card goes back to a draft for the person, a drill log reopens to its driller ("Sent back to you" on their home). Fix it and file that paper again; the office sees "refiled · waiting".

## What to do

1. Open the day and read the note.
2. Fix what it names — nothing else changes.
3. File again. This is **version 2**; the office sees both versions and the note, so the history is clear.

## When it is approved

The day reads "Approved 9:12 am by Evette" at the top, and Records shows who approved each paper.
