Filing turns the day into the record the office keeps: a signed PDF of the blasting log and the daily report. Until you file, the day is yours to change. After, it is locked.

## Before you file

1. Every shot has its **responsible blaster's signature**. Open the blasting log, scroll to the shot's sign-off and sign on the screen. Your name and license come from your profile.
2. The daily report has the crew and their hours. Anyone who worked and is missing will not be on the paperwork.
3. Look at the compliance badges on each shot. An amber or red badge is not a stop — but it is worth a note in the shot before you file, because the office will ask.

![Continue reads "File this day"](work-day.png)

## Filing

Tap **Continue** on the day, or open the day and tap **File this day**. You get a preview of both PDFs. Anything missing is listed above the button — fix it from there, you do not need to hunt for it.

Tap **File**. The day shows **Filed**, the PDFs are stored, and the office sees the day in its queue.

> **No signal?** File anyway. The PDFs are made on your device and upload on their own when you have a connection again. The day shows "Filed · waiting to upload" until then.

![The file screen](file-day.png)

## After filing

- The day is locked. You can read it, print it and see its PDFs under **My records**.
- Found a mistake? Ask the office to **send it back**. It returns to your Dashboard with their note; fix it and file again — that becomes version 2, and both versions are kept.
- The office may also send it back on its own. You will see the note on the day.
