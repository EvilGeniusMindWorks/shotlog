A seismograph reading turns the design's prediction into a measurement. One reading per instrument per shot.

## Entering a reading

1. From the day, open **Seismo**, or tap the shot's seismo line.
2. Enter the instrument, its distance from the shot, and the readings: peak particle velocity for each axis, frequency, and air overpressure.
3. Tap the camera to photograph the printout. The photo is kept with the reading, so the paper can stay in the truck.

![Seismo readings](seismo.png)

## What ShotLog does with it

The measured PPV and frequency are checked against USBM RI 8507 and OSM, and the reading's badge turns green, amber or red. Over time, the measured readings at a site calibrate its **K factor**: from the job page you can apply a calibrated K to the site, and every job there inherits it.
