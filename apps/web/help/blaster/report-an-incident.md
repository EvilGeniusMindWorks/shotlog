An incident is a paper of the work day, like the daily report: it carries the day's job, customer and site, shows on the day's **Incidents** tile, and files on its own the moment you send it — the office sees it within minutes, whether or not the day is filed.

## Where to report one

- **The day's Incidents tile** — "None today · Report". Tap Report, pick the kind, and the report opens with the day already on it.
- **The home's + button** — Start a day at a job, or **Report an incident**. It asks which day (today's is first) or "Not tied to a work day" for a truck accident on the road, theft at the yard.
- **The day's ⋯ menu** still has **Report an incident** for a blasting complaint: it links the shot and the nearest seismo reading for you.

## The kinds

Blasting complaint or damage claim · Utility strike · Equipment or vehicle · **Injury** · **Near miss** · Other. Injury and Near miss have their own questions and their own printed report: who was hurt, where on the site, what they were doing, the injury and body part, the cause, the treatment and facility, witnesses, lost time; for a near miss, what stopped it, the hazard, what should change, who was told.

## Do now

The list at the top, in red, is the job's contact sheet turned into steps for this kind of incident: call 911 for anything serious, the hospital or urgent care, the office's Injury or Incident contact, the utility, the police on a public way. **Call** opens your phone's dialler with the number and records that you did it, with the time; **Done** records a plain confirmation. The app never listens to the call — the tap is your word, with the time, and it prints on the report as "Who was told, and when". A step whose contact is not on the job's sheet yet says so.

## Send it

Fill in what happened and the kind's questions, add photos, then **Send to Office**. The point-in-time report files with its photos, the office's Incidents list picks it up for review, and the tile on the day reads "1 · Injury 10:42 · sent to the office". Closing an incident is the office's decision.
