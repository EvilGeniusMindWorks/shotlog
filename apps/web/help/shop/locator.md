Where every machine was last recorded being used — from the crews' own checklists, drill logs and daily reports, with no tracker on the machine.

## Reading it

A pin per site on the map, a list beside it (a toggle on a phone). Each machine shows the job and site it was last seen at and how long ago. Grey chips are **stale** readings — nobody has recorded that machine for a while.

![Locator](locator.png)

## Back at the yard

When a machine comes home, tap **Mark at the yard**. The next field record that names it moves it out again on its own.
