Your home is the queue: what is down, what has an open ticket, what is due for service — and one worklist in the order you want.

## The three numbers

- **Down** — machines out of service right now.
- **Tickets** — open repair tickets, from rig checklists and from you.
- **Due soon** — services due by the hour ledger.

**Checklists filed today** shows how many rigs the field has looked at.

![My Shop](shop.png)

## One worklist, your order

Tickets and due services in a single list. The default order is sensible — downs first, then oldest — but you know what the app cannot: which job is waiting on which machine. **Drag** rows into the order you will work them; it sticks. **Reset** puts the default back.

Open a row to resolve the ticket or log the service.
