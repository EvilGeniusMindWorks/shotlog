A machine's page is its whole story: hours, tickets, services, and where it last worked.

## Where hours come from

Every reading is a ledger entry with its source, who, and when: a driller's checklist starting hours, the end-of-day meter at sign-complete, equipment hours on a daily report, and your corrections. The current hours are the latest of these — never a number typed over.

## Correcting a meter

When the physical meter disagrees with the app, enter a **correction** on the machine's page: the observed value, with your name and the time. The old reading stays in the ledger; the correction wins from then on.

![A machine's page](machine.png)

## Services and PM due

**Log a service** when one is done — engine, compressor, whichever — with the hours it was done at. The PM clock for that service restarts from there, and the **Due soon** count on your home follows the hour ledger. The intervals are the company's assumptions until the shop confirms them.
