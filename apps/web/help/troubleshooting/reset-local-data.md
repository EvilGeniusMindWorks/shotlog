Reset local data throws away this device's copy of the company and downloads it again. It fixes a copy that got stuck. It does not touch anything already sent to the server.

## Before you tap it

The panel tells you if changes are still queued — those would be lost. If it says so and you have signal, wait for **Synced** first. If there is no signal, wait for signal.

## How

Tap the sync chip → **Reset local data** → confirm. The app clears the copy, reloads, and shows **Downloading company data** with a percentage. Sign in stays; your PIN stays.

> **Taking too long?** The panel offers a hint after a minute. On a very old copy the reset finishes at the next launch — close the app fully and open it again.
