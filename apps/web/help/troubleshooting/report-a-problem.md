Tap **?** → **Send feedback** on the screen where the problem is. A couple of words is plenty. A screenshot and the screen's name — the job, the date, the shot or the rig when the screen has one — go with it automatically, and the sheet shows the name it will send; it works without signal and sends when you have some.

It goes to Matthew, who builds ShotLog. If the app crashed, it offers **Report** on the spot — tap it and the details go too.
