## "Invalid credentials"

The email or password is wrong, or the account was deactivated. Check the email first — it is the one your invitation went to. Then **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen: a reset link is emailed and you choose a new one. If email is not set up for your company yet, the screen says so — ask your admin for a temporary password instead.

## The invitation link does not work

It works once and expires in a few days. Ask whoever invited you to send a new one.

## Signed in, but the app asks for a PIN I do not know

See [I forgot my PIN](/help/troubleshooting/forgot-pin).

## It signed me out on its own

Your account was moved to another company (this happens at go-live) — sign in again and the device downloads the new one. Or the session expired after a long time away: sign in again; nothing on the device is lost.
