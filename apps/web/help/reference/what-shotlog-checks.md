The compliance badges on a shot compare the predicted and measured values against published limits.

## USBM RI 8507

The frequency-dependent limit on peak particle velocity: lower at low frequencies where houses respond most, rising to 2.0 in/s from about 40 Hz. A measured reading's PPV and frequency are placed on that curve.

## OSMRE

Distance-based limits on PPV — 1.25 in/s within 300 ft, 1.0 in/s to 5,000 ft, 0.75 in/s beyond — and scaled-distance factors when no seismograph is used.

## The badges

Green is inside the limit, amber is close, red is over. A red badge does not stop the shot; it is a fact for the record, and worth a note. The **Reference** page in the app shows both curves.

## What is not checked

State and local limits (a town bylaw's PPV, for instance) are recorded on the site and shown in the shot designer, but the badge does not enforce them. Air overpressure is recorded, not checked. The engineer of record's judgement governs.
