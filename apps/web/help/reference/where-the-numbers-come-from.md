- **USBM RI 8507** — Siskind et al., *Structure Response and Damage Produced by Ground Vibration from Surface Mine Blasting*, U.S. Bureau of Mines, 1980. The frequency-dependent "safe level" curve.
- **OSMRE** — 30 CFR 816.67, the surface mining regulations' distance-based PPV limits and scaled-distance factors.
- **Scaled distance and the propagation law** — the square-root scaling and the K × SD^-1.6 relationship are the industry's standard site-law form; the exponent is the conventional value, the K factor is measured per site.
- **Product weights** — from each manufacturer's data sheets, entered in the catalog by the office.

The formulas live in one shared module used by the app on the device and by the server, so a number is the same wherever you read it.
