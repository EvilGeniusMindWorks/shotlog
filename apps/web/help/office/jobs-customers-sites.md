The hierarchy is Customer › Site › Job, and the office keeps it true: contacts, terms, permits, addresses.

## What lives where

| On the customer | On the site | On the job |
|---|---|---|
| Company & billing, contacts, COI, terms, PO required, tax status | Address and state, Site K, rock and ground, jurisdiction and permits, local limits, access and safety notes, jobsite and emergency contacts | Number, name, PO, status, dates, engineer of record, operation, defaults for the day |

Every day at a job inherits all of it, so a blaster never types an address or a K factor.

## Keeping it current

**Jobs** lands on customers. Open a customer for its About cards and sites; open a site for its About cards and jobs. **+ New** at each level makes the thing at that level with its parent already set. Job numbers are assigned automatically per year and can be edited on the job page.
