Complaints, utility strikes and asset incidents are filed in the field and processed here.

## The list

**Admin › Incidents** shows open incidents, claims in review, and closed ones. Open one to work it.

## Working a claim

The incident page holds what the field recorded — kind, job, site, date, what happened, photos — and the claim fields you fill: status, amount, notes. **Closing** an incident is an office decision; the field cannot close its own.
