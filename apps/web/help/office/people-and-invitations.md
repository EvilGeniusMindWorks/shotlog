**Admin › People** is the roster: everyone who works here, with or without a login.

## Adding a person

**Add person** with their name and role. That is enough for them to appear on crews and time cards. A login comes with an invitation.

## Inviting

On the person's row, **Invite**: enter their email and send. They get the invitation email with a one-time link (it expires in a few days). Without email, **share the link** yourself — by text, in person. When they set a password the login is theirs and their roster entry links to it.

![People](people.png)

## Later

- **Reset password** — sends a temporary one; they choose their own at the next sign-in.
- **Deactivate** — the login stops working; the records stay. Accounts are never deleted.
