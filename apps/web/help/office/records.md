Records is the company's record book: every filed day, drill log, checklist and incident, with its PDF, searchable in seconds.

## Finding things

The facets on the left count as you go: kind, status (draft · filed, awaiting approval · approved · sent back), job, customer, site, person, dates. The search box takes anything — a job number, a town, a name. **Group by** date, job or kind.

![Records](records-office.png)

## Reading a record

Tap a row: the PDF opens in the preview pane (a sheet on a phone). The preview also shows who filed it, the **version chain** when a day was refiled, and the file's integrity (SHA-256, size, where the copy lives). A live record opens as a summary card with **Open live record**.

## Getting records out

Select rows — or **Select all in group** — then:

- **Download ZIP** — a folder per job and date, with an index.csv listing every file and its checksum.
- **CSV index** — the list alone, for a spreadsheet.
- **Print** — opens the PDFs for printing (six at a time).

> **"Not reachable from this device"** means the PDF copy is on the server and this device is offline. It downloads when you have signal.
