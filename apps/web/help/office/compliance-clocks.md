Two things expire quietly: a customer's certificate of insurance and a site's permits. ShotLog counts them down.

## Where they live

- **Customer › Compliance & terms** — the COI expiry date, W-9 on file, payment terms, PO required.
- **Site › Jurisdiction & permits** — each permit with its number, authority and expiry.

Each shows a countdown pill: green, amber inside 30 days, red when past.

## Where they surface

Your home's **Expiring** section lists anything within 90 days, so you do not have to go looking. The blaster sees the same countdown on the site's About cards when they open a job there.
