Your home is the work that needs an office decision, oldest first, and the clocks that are about to run out.

## Today's jobs

Above your queues sits Today's jobs: one row per job with a day happening today — its work code (DB, DO, DE, C, H), who's on site, and four dots for its papers (blasting log, daily report, drilling, time cards). Grey means not started, amber in progress, green filed or accepted, teal approved, red sent back. Rows that need you float to the top — a day sent back, a drill log signed complete and waiting on the blaster, nothing started by mid-morning. A **Needs you / All today** filter and a search box (job, customer or person) sit above the list. Tap a row to open that day's tiles read-only: you can look at any paper there, but approving still happens from the queues below.

## The sections

- **Awaiting approval** — filed days, each with what came with it (shots, pounds, crew, time cards, and "filed with 2 notes" when the blaster filed past the pre-flight) and **Review**. Approving and sending back are the supervisor's and the admin's; you read the queue, open the day, and download or send its PDF — the Approvals page says so at the top.
- **Sent back, waiting** — days you returned that the blaster has not refiled.
- **Time cards to approve** — grouped by day and job; **Approve all** when they match the day.
- **Expiring** — certificates of insurance and permits within 90 days.
- **Incidents** — open claims.
- **Never submitted** — a day worked more than three days ago that was never filed.
