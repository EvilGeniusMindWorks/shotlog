**Admin › Approvals** is the pending list: filed days with real columns — job, date, who filed it, the papers (5 · 1 sent back), how long it has waited. **Mine** shows the days with a paper waiting that your role approves; **All** shows everything.

## Who approves what

**Admin › Company › Approvals** is the matrix: for each kind of paper (blasting log, daily report, drill log, rig checklist, time cards, incident, repair ticket), which roles approve it and send it back. It ships with the office ticked on every paper; admin approves everything. A tick here is the same as a tick on the Roles page, so the server enforces exactly what the grid shows. The last row decides whether a day is approved **as one** (Approve the day covers every paper not sent back) or **paper by paper** (Approve the day waits until each paper is approved).

## The review screen

**Review** opens the day: the papers down the left — blasting log, daily report, drill logs, rig checklists, time cards, incidents — with where each stands (filed · waiting, accepted by the blaster, approved by Evette 9:12 am, sent back), and the selected paper's PDF with its attachments on the right. The header names who filed it, the crew and the rigs.

On each paper: **Approve**, or **Send back…** with a note. A time card sent back goes back to the person as a draft with your note; a drill log reopens to its driller ("Sent back to you" on their home); a checklist carries the note. The blaster's day reads "1 paper sent back · Time card · Lisa Vital" on its File row, and the paper shows on their Dashboard under Sent back.

**Approve the day** approves the day; it waits while any paper is sent back. **Send the day back…** returns the whole day with a note, as before. **Print pack** and **Export binder** take this day.

## What the filer sees

The day reads "Approved 9:12 am by Evette" at the top; Records shows Approved by on each paper (turn the column on from Columns). A sent-back paper shows the note where it is fixed.

## Reopening

An approved day can be reopened from the review screen by an approver — the filer cannot; it is a legal record once approved. Every approve, send-back and reopen is in the audit trail with who and when.
