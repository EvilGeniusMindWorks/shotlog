**Admin › Approvals** is the pending list: filed days, oldest first, with the facts attached.

## Approve

**Review** opens the day and its PDFs. **Approve** locks the day for good and moves it to the approved list.

## Send back

**Send back** asks what needs fixing — one line the blaster will read on their Dashboard: "shot 2 not signed", "Ray missing from the crew". The day unlocks for them; when they file again it is version 2, and both versions stay with your note.

![Approvals](approvals.png)

## Reopening

An approved day can be unlocked from the day itself if it truly must change. Every approve, send-back and unlock is in the audit trail with who and when.
